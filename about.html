Independent technology coverage

Technology,
made clearer.

CORE is an independent technology blog by Iian Kehn, focused on the devices, software, and services people use every day.

Coverage spans consumer technology in all its forms: phones, computers, operating systems, accessories, smart-home products, cloud services, and the companies shaping how those products evolve.

CORE looks beyond specifications and launch-day excitement to explain what a development means in practical terms. The goal is straightforward reporting that helps readers understand what changed, why it matters, and how it may affect the technology they already own.

News reports are independently written from clearly credited sources, with links to the original reporting for readers who want the complete context. Commentary and longer features favor useful detail over noise, hype, or endless scrolling.

About Iian

Iian Kehn is a technology enthusiast and writer based in Omaha, Nebraska. His interests include consumer electronics, personal computing, mobile technology, operating systems, cloud services, and the ways technology fits into everyday life.

CORE is built and maintained by hand using plain HTML and CSS. That simplicity keeps the site fast, accessible, and focused on the writing.

Editorial approach: CORE values clear attribution, independent writing, practical context, and an honest distinction between confirmed news, informed analysis, and opinion.
