About this place

Less noise.
More signal.

CORE is an independent archive by Iian Kehn about technology, work, and the systems beneath both.

It is a place to slow ideas down and look at what actually matters: how a tool behaves, why a process breaks, what a change means for the people living with it, and which lessons remain useful after the novelty fades.

The site is intentionally simple. There are no trackers, feeds, pop-ups, or complicated layers between the writing and the reader. Every page is made with ordinary HTML and CSS.

About Iian

Iian Kehn works with technology and writes from Omaha, Nebraska. His interests sit where practical computing, clear communication, and thoughtful systems meet.
