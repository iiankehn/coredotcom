Cities Are Rethinking What Happens After Dark

September 10, 2026 · 6 min read

Urban planning has traditionally focused heavily on daytime activity. But transportation, public safety, business, and culture continue long after offices close.

City leaders are increasingly treating nighttime as its own planning challenge, with different needs and patterns of movement.

The night is part of the city

Better late-night transportation and thoughtful public spaces can affect workers, residents, visitors, and businesses. The goal is not simply to make cities busier, but to make them more functional throughout the full day.
