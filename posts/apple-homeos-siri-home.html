Apple’s rumored homeOS puts Siri at the center of the home

A reported October launch would bring Apple’s widgets, apps, and voice assistant together on a new smart-home display.

By Iian KehnOctober 2, 20263 min read

Apple may be ready to give the smart home an operating system of its own.

A new report says Apple plans to introduce a tabletop home hub on October 13 alongside software known as homeOS. Neither the product nor the date has been confirmed by Apple, but the details point to a more ambitious home strategy than the company’s current collection of Home apps, speakers, and television boxes.

The rumored device, sometimes called HomePad, is expected to combine a touch display with Siri. Its interface reportedly borrows from several familiar Apple products: iPhone-style widgets, iPad-like apps, and an icon-driven Home Screen reminiscent of Apple Watch.

A screen designed to stay visible

When it is not being actively used, the display could emphasize photos and clocks in a layout similar to StandBy on iPhone. That makes the product less like a tablet waiting to be picked up and more like a shared surface for a kitchen, desk, or living room.

The expected built-in software covers the essentials of a family hub, including Music, Podcasts, Photos, Weather, FaceTime, Notes, Calendar, Messages, and Reminders. A dedicated App Store is not expected at launch. Third-party information may instead arrive through widgets supplied by nearby iPhones.

Siri is the pivotal piece. Apple can already control lights, locks, scenes, and media by voice, but a persistent display would give those requests visible results and make smart-home status easier to understand at a glance. Touch controls would provide a fallback when speaking is inconvenient.

If the report proves accurate, homeOS will be less about inventing a new category than unifying Apple’s existing strengths. The opportunity is to turn scattered features into one approachable household interface—and to show whether Apple’s renewed work on Siri is ready for the most shared screen in the home.
