Apple Music hides a playable iPod in its error page

A browser tribute brings the original scroll-wheel experience back for the iPod’s 25th anniversary.

By Iian KehnOctober 23, 20261 min read

Apple has turned a dead end on its music website into a small celebration of the iPod. An interactive version of the original player appears when visitors reach a nonexistent Apple Music address.

9to5Mac reports that the buttons work and the scroll wheel responds to clicking and dragging. Selecting a song plays a preview in the browser, recreating part of the experience without bringing back the hardware.

The tribute arrived October 23, exactly 25 years after Apple introduced the first iPod in 2001.

It is a fitting place for the old interface to reappear. A device once built around a locally stored music collection now lives inside a streaming service, preserving the pleasure of browsing with a wheel.
