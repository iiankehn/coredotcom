Apple TV 4K turns Siri into a hands-free screen

The fourth-generation box adds an A19 chip, far-field microphones, a full-screen Siri AI experience, and four-speaker surround sound.

By Iian KehnOctober 13, 20263 min read

Apple TV 4K is becoming less like a streaming box with a voice shortcut and more like a room-aware computer for the television.

The fourth-generation Apple TV 4K introduces Siri AI as a full-screen, conversational interface. Three built-in far-field microphones let viewers speak without holding the remote, while echo cancellation is designed to pick out a voice even when the television is playing.

The new model costs $299, is available to preorder today, and reaches stores November 13. Siri AI launches in beta on Apple TV 4K models from the second generation onward, though the new hardware is the version built around hands-free use.

Search becomes a conversation

Siri AI can search across entertainment, answer follow-up questions about what is on screen, and use broader world knowledge without covering the program with a large interface. Apple’s examples include asking for Emmy-winning shows, learning where a series was filmed, or checking whether a title is appropriate for a teenager.

When paired with an Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone, the television can also draw on personal context. A request to play the show a friend recommended can use information from a message instead of requiring the viewer to remember the title.

That intelligence is backed by Apple’s A19 chip. Apple claims up to 50 percent faster CPU performance and twice the GPU performance of the previous generation, plus a dedicated AV1 decoder for more efficient high-quality streaming. The Siri Remote keeps its familiar shape but adds Find My, Precision Finding on supported iPhones, and the option to make the remote play a sound.

A more ambitious home-theater hub

Apple TV 4K can now coordinate four speakers for surround sound. The setup requires the fourth-generation box and matched front and rear pairs of either HomePod mini 2 or second-generation HomePod speakers. The devices identify their positions, then calibrate timing and levels for the room.

The box is also leaning further into its role as a home hub. Doorbell alerts can appear over a program, camera events can receive Apple Intelligence-generated descriptions and notification summaries, and accessories or scenes can be controlled through Siri. Wi-Fi and Thread are built in.

Apple says microphone data is handled inside protected Secure Exclaves on the A19 and cannot be accessed by tvOS, apps, the user, or Apple. Siri requests can be processed locally or through Private Cloud Compute, while HomeKit Secure Video remains end-to-end encrypted.

The price puts this Apple TV far above basic streaming sticks. Its argument is that the television is no longer the whole product: Siri, HomePods, cameras, games, FaceTime, and smart-home controls are being assembled into a single living-room system.
