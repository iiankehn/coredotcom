Apple sets October 13 for its “Welcome home” event

The confirmed event points toward Apple’s next smart-home chapter, but the devices themselves remain unannounced until next week.

By Iian KehnOctober 8, 20262 min read

Apple has put a date on its next move in the home: October 13.

The company teased a special event with the phrase “Welcome home,” while press invitations point to an Apple Experience in Tribeca, New York at 9 a.m. Eastern. The wording leaves little doubt about the category Apple wants people watching, even if the company has not yet named the products.

Expectations center on a refreshed Apple TV 4K, a second-generation HomePod mini, and the long-rumored HomePad display. Reports have also linked Apple with LG on a wider collection of home accessories, though those products remain part of the rumor picture rather than the confirmed announcement.

A product family, not one device

The significant idea is not any single box or speaker. Apple appears positioned to present the home as a coordinated platform, with screens, audio, television, Siri, and automation working as one system.

A dedicated display could give that platform a visible center. The Apple TV can anchor entertainment, while smaller speakers extend voice and audio through individual rooms. If Apple can make those pieces feel coherent, “Welcome home” may describe an ecosystem reset more than a routine hardware refresh.

The event format is still unclear. Apple has confirmed the date and distributed press invitations, but it has not said whether viewers will get a full-length online presentation. The company used a similar in-person experience around the MacBook Neo earlier this year after a sequence of press-release announcements.

For now, the safest conclusion is also the most interesting one: Apple has formally chosen the home for its next stage. The details arrive October 13.
