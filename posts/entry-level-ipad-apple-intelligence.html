Apple Intelligence may be headed to the entry-level iPad

References found in Apple’s code point to an A19 chip, more memory, and faster connectivity for its most affordable tablet.

By Iian KehnSeptember 25, 20262 min read

Apple’s least expensive iPad could be preparing for a meaningful internal upgrade, bringing the company’s on-device intelligence features to the bottom of the tablet lineup.

References discovered in Apple’s code identify an A19 chip and 8GB of memory for a future 12th-generation iPad. That combination would satisfy the hardware requirements for Apple Intelligence and its Siri features.

The change would be larger than a routine speed increase. Apple Intelligence support would place the entry-level model on the same software path as Apple’s more expensive tablets, removing a capability divide created by the current hardware.

Connectivity is part of the upgrade

The code also points to Apple’s C1X modem, which supports sub-6GHz 5G, and the N1 networking chip. N1 would add Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, and Thread support.

Apple has not announced the tablet, its price, or a release date. Current rumors cited by AppleInsider suggest a spring 2027 launch and a starting price that could remain near the current model’s $449 entry point.

If those details hold, the next base iPad would become less of a separate budget tier and more of an accessible route into Apple’s current intelligence and connectivity platform.

Original reporting
