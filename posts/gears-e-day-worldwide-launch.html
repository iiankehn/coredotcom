Gears of War: E-Day starts its worldwide campaign

The long-running series returns with an action-horror prequel, three-player campaign co-op, and a larger cooperative Horde mode.

By Iian KehnOctober 6, 20262 min read

Gears of War is returning to its beginning—and to the center of Xbox’s fall lineup.

Gears of War: E-Day has launched worldwide on Xbox and PC after an early-access period that began October 1. It is also available through Xbox Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass, giving subscribers immediate access to the series’ first new mainline entry of the current console generation.

The prequel leans into action horror, revisiting the moment the Locust first erupts from underground. That shift pushes the campaign toward a more ominous tone while retaining the heavy cover-based combat that established the franchise.

Built to be played together

The campaign supports cooperative play for as many as three people. Multiplayer also crosses the Xbox-PC divide, reducing the usual friction of finding friends on the same platform.

Traditional Versus matches remain, but the larger addition is Horde Siege, a player-versus-environment mode for up to 12 people organized across three squads. The expanded format turns the familiar survival loop into a broader coordination challenge.

The launch arrives 20 years after the original game debuted on Xbox 360. That history gives E-Day two jobs: it must work as an accessible origin story and prove that one of Xbox’s defining series can still feel urgent.

Its Game Pass release lowers the barrier to finding out. For Xbox, the significance is larger than one sequel: E-Day is a prominent test of whether a familiar franchise, refreshed around cooperative play and a darker identity, can pull attention back toward its platform.
