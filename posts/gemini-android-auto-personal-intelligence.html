Gemini on Android Auto can now draw on your Gmail, Calendar, and Photos

Personal Intelligence is rolling out on Android Auto now, with cars that have Google built-in following in the coming months.

By Iian KehnOctober 20, 20263 min read

Voice assistants in cars have mostly needed exact instructions: a full address, a contact name, a specific song. Google's latest Gemini update aims to fill in those details from the rest of your Google account.

Google announced on October 20 that Personal Intelligence is starting to roll out for Gemini on Android Auto. The feature lets Gemini use information from connected Google services, including Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Photos, Google Maps, YouTube, and Search, to answer requests that would otherwise need more detail.

Shorter requests, more context

Google's examples show the idea. A driver can ask for their flight's gate and whether it's on time in one request, and Gemini can find the booking in Gmail. Asking for directions to a doctor's appointment works without knowing the address, because the appointment is on the calendar. A photo of a car wash taken last week can become a navigation destination, and a coffee shop from an earlier Gemini conversation can be picked up again.

Preferences carry through as well. Google says Gemini can look for an indoor restaurant along a route that fits a person's usual choices, such as vegetarian options, without being told again.

Opt-in, and not everywhere

Personal Intelligence is off unless you turn it on. Apps aren't connected until you enable the feature, and it can be switched off in Gemini settings. If you have already enabled it in the Gemini app on your phone, Android Auto picks it up automatically. Without it, Gemini in the car still handles navigation, music, calls, and general questions; the answers just aren't personalized.

Eligibility is narrower than Gemini's in-car rollout overall. Google says Personal Intelligence requires a personal Google account and an age of 18 or older; Workspace business, enterprise, and education accounts are excluded. It is rolling out globally except in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and Nigeria.

Built-in cars come later

Cars with Google built-in, which run Android Automotive directly rather than projecting from a phone, will get Personal Intelligence in the coming months. In those vehicles, Google says personalized responses are only shared when the owner's phone is connected in the car, which limits what the car itself can reveal to anyone else who drives it.

The update moves Gemini closer to the assistant drivers were promised years ago: one that understands what you mean, not just what you say. It also means the car becomes another place where Google's AI reads your inbox and calendar, a tradeoff Google is leaving to each driver to accept.
