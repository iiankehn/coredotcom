Ask Maps adds shareable trip itineraries

Google’s November 16 update also searches saved lists and expands across Europe.

By Iian KehnNovember 16, 20261 min read

Google Maps is rolling out new Ask Maps trip-planning features on Android and iOS, Google announced November 16.

Users can request a day outing or a multi-day road trip and receive a schedule with numbered map pins for each stop. Plans can be shared or exported to Google Docs on desktop.

Ask Maps also searches saved lists and checks availability at bookmarked places. Itineraries can be adjusted conversationally while traveling.

The rollout expands Ask Maps to 30 more European countries, including Spain, Germany, and the United Kingdom. Itinerary and list tools work wherever Ask Maps is offered; route tips and appointment-finding launch first in the United States.
