iPhone Duo puts Control Center within easier reach

A tap on the status icons offers an alternative to reaching for the top-right corner.

By Iian KehnOctober 23, 20261 min read

Apple’s folding iPhone has a useful navigation detail: tapping its combined battery, Wi-Fi, and cellular indicator opens Control Center.

9to5Mac highlights the shortcut through a demonstration by Stephen Robles. It provides another route to the controls normally reached with a swipe from the top-right corner, a stretch that can feel awkward when holding the wide device in the left hand.

When a Live Activity is running, the indicator sits farther down the display, bringing the target closer still.

It is a small change with a practical payoff. A larger screen creates more room for content, but everyday controls also need to remain reachable. Here, a familiar status indicator doubles as an entry point.
