iFixit finds repair gains and unanswered hinge questions in iPhone Duo

A provisional repairability score comes alongside possible explanations for early display failures.

By Iian KehnOctober 25, 20261 min read

Apple’s first folding phone is easier to repair in some important respects, but its hinge and inner screen remain the central concern in a new iFixit teardown.

Reported by 9to5Mac, the assessment gives iPhone Duo a provisional 5 out of 10 for repairability, pending replacement-parts availability. iFixit praises electrically releasable battery adhesive and an outer screen that is relatively straightforward to remove.

The investigation also considers whether hinge components could concentrate stress near the display areas where owners have reported damage. A support bar or guide mechanism moving incorrectly is among the possibilities.

These are investigative leads, rather than a confirmed diagnosis or a measurement of how widespread failures are. The teardown’s encouraging repair choices and unresolved durability questions can both be true: easier disassembly does not establish long-term reliability.
