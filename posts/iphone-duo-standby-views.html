iPhone Duo code points to five more StandBy views

Localization strings reveal possible camera, smart-home, art, and Snoopy experiences that Apple has not announced.

By Iian KehnSeptember 28, 20262 min read

Apple may have more planned for the iPhone Duo’s StandBy mode than it revealed during the device’s introduction.

Newly uncovered localization strings in Apple’s software point to five additional views designed around the foldable phone’s standing configuration.

The names discovered in the code are Home Camera, Home Modular, Flow, Fade, and Snoopy. Home Camera appears intended to show either one security-camera feed or a grid of feeds, while Home Modular could provide customizable smart-home controls.

More than a bedside clock

Flow and Fade appear to be visual experiences. Snoopy may bring the character-driven style of Apple’s existing screensavers to the phone.

These additions would join the StandBy views Apple has already demonstrated, including analog and digital clocks, weather, photos, calendars, shared albums, and modular layouts. Unlike StandBy on earlier iPhones, the Duo’s standing mode can remain active without the phone being connected to a charger.

The information comes from software strings and a third-party examination of Apple’s code, not a new announcement from Apple. Names, designs, or availability could therefore change before release.

If the views ship as they currently appear, they would help turn the folded device into a small smart-home panel, photo display, or bedside screen when it is not being used as a phone. The iPhone Duo is scheduled to arrive October 23.

Original reporting
