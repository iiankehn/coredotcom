Microsoft’s Exchange transition is disrupting Apple’s Mac apps

Mail, Calendar, Contacts, Notes, and Reminders still depend on a connection Microsoft has begun turning off for work and school accounts.

By Iian KehnOctober 2, 20263 min read

A long-planned Microsoft infrastructure change is now becoming a visible problem for some Mac users.

Microsoft began disabling Exchange Web Services, or EWS, for Exchange Online in October. Apple’s built-in Mail, Calendar, Contacts, Notes, and Reminders apps on macOS rely on that older connection for many work and school accounts, which means access can fail as organizations move through the shutdown.

The impact depends on an organization’s configuration, so not every Exchange user will lose service at once. Administrators can temporarily allow approved applications to keep using EWS, but that is only a bridge: Microsoft plans to complete the retirement in April 2027.

A Mac-specific gap

The interruption does not affect the same Apple apps on iPhone and iPad because those devices connect through Exchange ActiveSync rather than EWS. On the Mac, Apple says a future macOS 27 update will migrate all five affected apps to Microsoft’s replacement technology.

Apple has not given a date for that update. Until it arrives—or until an organization grants a temporary exception—affected users may need to switch to Microsoft Outlook. Old Dominion University’s IT department is among the administrators recommending that path for its community.

This is the awkward middle of a security and platform transition. Microsoft needs to retire an aging interface, while Apple needs to move several deeply integrated apps without breaking the experience people expect from a system account. The deadlines were known, but the fix has not reached users before the cutoff began.

For Mac owners, the practical advice is simple: if an Exchange Online account suddenly stops syncing across several Apple apps, the password may not be the problem. Check with the organization’s IT team before repeatedly removing or rebuilding the account.
