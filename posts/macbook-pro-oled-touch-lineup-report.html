Report points to a premium OLED touchscreen tier for MacBook Pro

Apple’s expected laptop changes remain unconfirmed, including the proposed lineup and pricing.

By Iian KehnOctober 25, 20261 min read

Apple could keep familiar MacBook Pro designs on sale while adding a more expensive OLED touchscreen tier, according to a new 9to5Mac preview of the expected lineup.

The report anticipates three levels: a base 14-inch model with M6, existing M5 Pro and M5 Max machines, and redesigned touch-enabled models using those higher-end chips.

It also cites expectations for lighter designs. None of this should be read as Apple’s final specification sheet: the report is a pre-announcement account, and its proposed prices for the redesign are explicitly estimates.

If that structure materializes, touch and OLED would become premium differentiators rather than universal MacBook Pro features. Until Apple announces the products, the precise configurations, availability, and cost remain unsettled.
