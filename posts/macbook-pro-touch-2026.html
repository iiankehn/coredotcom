Touch arrives on MacBook Pro with a tandem OLED display

Announced October 28, Apple’s new premium laptops start at $2,899 and arrive November 6.

By Iian KehnOctober 28, 20261 min read

Apple announced its first touchscreen MacBook Pro on October 28, 2026. Available in 14- and 16-inch versions, the redesigned laptops pair M5 Pro or M5 Max with a tandem OLED screen branded PROLED XDR.

Apple specifies up to 2000 nits peak HDR and SDR brightness and adaptive refresh rates from 10Hz to 120Hz. Touch supports actions such as scrolling, pinching to zoom, and moving content, alongside the keyboard and trackpad.

Dynamic Island brings Spotlight, Live Activities, and Siri AI into a new area of the display. The thinner, lighter designs also include Thunderbolt 5 connectivity.

U.S. M5 Pro configurations start at $2,899 for 14 inches and $3,399 for 16 inches. Pre-orders opened October 28, with deliveries and store availability beginning November 6.

These are separate models from the M6 14-inch MacBook Pro announced October 27.
