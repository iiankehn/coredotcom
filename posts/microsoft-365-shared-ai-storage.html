Microsoft 365 is sharing the AI—and the storage limit

Family and Premium plans will extend Copilot features to every member, but OneDrive capacity is moving from individual allotments to a household pool.

By Iian KehnOctober 9, 20263 min read

Microsoft is making its consumer subscriptions more communal: the benefits get broader, while the limits become something a household manages together.

Microsoft has begun telling Microsoft 365 consumer subscribers that Copilot and other AI features will expand beyond the plan owner. Every member of a Family or Premium subscription will gain access to the included AI tools, according to reporting from Thurrott.

Those benefits include higher-use Copilot chat and image creation, Copilot inside Microsoft’s productivity apps, and other plan features. AI usage will draw from a shared allowance, however, rather than giving each person a separate pool. Premium and Pro subscribers will be able to buy more credits if the household runs out.

OneDrive becomes a family pool

The larger tradeoff is storage. Microsoft 365 Family currently gives each member 1TB of OneDrive capacity. Under the new model, the plan will provide 2TB shared among the primary subscriber and as many as five additional members.

Microsoft 365 Premium will also receive a 2TB shared pool, while Microsoft 365 Pro will provide 5TB. Files do not become communal: Microsoft says each person can still see only their own files unless they explicitly share them. What changes is the meter underneath those private accounts.

That distinction makes the new model flexible for uneven households. One person can use more space without wasting capacity assigned to light users. But a six-person Family plan that fills the old individual allowances could move from as much as 6TB in aggregate to 2TB shared—a major reduction that demands attention.

There is time to audit

For existing subscribers, shared storage begins after the first renewal on or after May 2, 2027, unless an eligible plan change triggers it earlier. Microsoft says some qualifying Family and Premium customers in supported markets may receive bonus storage when the transition reaches their renewal.

Before then, families should check who is using the plan, how much space each account consumes, and whether large photo or backup libraries need a different home. The expanded AI access is tangible, but it arrives with a new household chore: watching a common storage budget.
