Motorola MA2 disappears from Amazon

Availability changes follow this week’s retail launch.

By Iian KehnNovember 13, 20261 min read

The Motorola MA2 wireless Android Auto adapter has disappeared from Amazon just days after going on sale, according to Ben Schoon’s November 13 report for 9to5Google.

This follows our November 11 coverage of its $69.99 U.S. launch. Today’s report changes the availability picture; the disappearance alone does not establish a recall or permanent cancellation.

Buyers should check current availability before relying on earlier delivery estimates.
