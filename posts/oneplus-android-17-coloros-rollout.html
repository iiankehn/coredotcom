OnePlus begins its stable Android 17 move to ColorOS

OnePlus 15 and Pad 4 lead an India-first rollout that replaces the OxygenOS name.

By Iian KehnOctober 23, 20261 min read

OnePlus has started distributing stable Android 17 software for the OnePlus 15 and OnePlus Pad 4 in India, according to 9to5Google. The update arrives as ColorOS 17, beginning the transition away from OxygenOS branding on existing devices.

The first releases precede the company’s previously reported October 29 start date. A further wave, including OnePlus 13, is scheduled for October 30.

For now, the initial rollout covers two devices. Owners elsewhere should not assume the same update is immediately available in their region.

OxygenOS had already grown closer to Oppo’s software. This release makes the shared identity explicit, while leaving availability dependent on the device and rollout stage.
