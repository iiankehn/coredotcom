Oppo’s next Find N foldable is headed for Europe

Today’s reporting points to broader distribution, with launch details still unsettled.

By Iian KehnNovember 6, 20261 min read

Oppo plans to bring its next Find N foldable to Europe, 9to5Google reported November 6, citing an interview with the company’s Bulgarian sales manager.

In the original Nixanbal interview coverage, Dimitar Mitov described plans for 2027. The outlet also noted that the final product name could differ from the expected Find N7 label.

That would expand the series’ official reach after the Find N6 missed European distribution despite its international introduction.

The reporting establishes a market plan rather than a finished launch schedule. A precise release date, European pricing, and final specifications remain unconfirmed; buyers should not treat rumored hardware details as announced features.

Sources
