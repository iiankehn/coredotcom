Microsoft · Field note 049

Outlook is retiring its Copilot inbox sorter

Removal begins October 26, with the rollout expected to finish by March 2027.

By Iian KehnOctober 23, 20261 min read

Microsoft is winding down Prioritize My Inbox, the Outlook feature that uses Copilot to highlight messages it considers most relevant to a person’s work.

Windows Central reports that removal starts October 26. An archived Microsoft notice says the change will reach all Outlook clients, with completion expected by the end of March 2027.

The feature adapts to working habits and can use personalized prompts to identify important mail. Microsoft says its retirement will simplify the Outlook Copilot experience and focus investment elsewhere; it has not supplied a detailed explanation.

No administrator action is required to trigger removal. Organizations that rely on the feature should tell affected users and update training material as the rollout progresses.
