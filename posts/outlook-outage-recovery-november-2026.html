Outlook outage recovery advances

Microsoft reports progress; some delays may remain.

By Iian KehnNovember 13, 20261 min read

Microsoft reported broad recovery on November 13 after the outage that began Thursday disrupted Outlook and other Microsoft 365 services.

The company says it addressed a network-upgrade issue exposed by a later change, and ruled out cyberattacks and AI agents. Restoration continued for remaining customers and regions.

Email delivery could still lag. This is a recovery update, rather than confirmation that every account is fully restored.

Sources
