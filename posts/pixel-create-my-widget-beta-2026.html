Pixel’s widget creator opens for beta testing

Eligible Pixel 10 and 11 owners can try the feature before its wider rollout.

By Iian KehnNovember 16, 20261 min read

Google has opened a Play Store beta for Create My Widget, 9to5Google reported November 16, giving eligible Pixel owners an earlier route to the feature.

Google’s support page says it works for adults on Pixel 10 and newer devices, excluding Pixel 10a, in English in Australia, Canada, India, the United Kingdom, and the United States.

Long-press an empty Home screen area, choose Widgets, then Create. Describe the information you want, review the generated preview, and add it to the screen.

Some functions need data-access permissions, and higher usage may require a subscription. Google also advises checking generated responses for accuracy.
