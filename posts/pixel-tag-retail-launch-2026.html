Pixel Tag goes on sale with a $29.99 starting price

Google’s Find Hub tracker arrives individually or in a discounted four-pack.

By Iian KehnNovember 11, 20261 min read

Google’s Pixel Tag is available to buy starting November 11, 2026, following its earlier unveiling. A single tracker costs $29.99, while four cost $99.99.

9to5Google reports availability through the Google Store and Amazon, with the four-pack initially appearing only at Google’s storefront.

The pill-shaped tracker has a replaceable CR2032 battery, rated for more than a year, along with Bluetooth 6.0, NFC, ultra-wideband, and IP67 water and dust resistance.

Launch markets are Australia, Canada, India, Japan, Singapore, Taiwan, the United Kingdom, and the United States excluding Puerto Rico. Bellroy and Spigen cases are sold separately.
