Sony brings transparent shells back with PS5’s Prism Collection

Orange, green, and gray accessories debut February 4, with pre-orders opening November 10.

By Iian KehnOctober 28, 20261 min read

Sony announced the Prism Collection on October 28, 2026, giving DualSense controllers and PS5 console covers translucent finishes in Flare Orange, Emerald Green, and Graphite Gray.

The design brings a familiar late-1990s hardware idea to the current PlayStation: let the shell reveal some of what sits underneath. Sony’s designers say the glossy treatment extends around the controller’s back, paired with a metallic middle section.

U.S. recommended prices are $84.99 per controller and $74.99 for matching console covers. Pre-orders open November 10, 2026, ahead of a rollout starting February 4, 2027. Regional availability and launch dates may vary.

It is a cosmetic refresh, with the appeal resting on color and material rather than a new controller feature.
