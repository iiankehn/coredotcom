Welcome to CORE

A home for deliberate writing in a web that rarely pauses.

By Iian KehnSeptember 17, 20264 min read

The internet does not need another stream of disposable thoughts. It could use more places built for ideas that have been considered, tested, and made clear.

That is the purpose of CORE. This site is a personal archive for observations about technology, work, and the often invisible systems that organize our days.

Why keep it simple?

A simple site asks very little of its reader. It loads quickly, works almost anywhere, and keeps the writing in the foreground. It also asks something useful of its author: make every page intentional.

The structure should support the thought, then get out of its way.

CORE uses plain HTML and CSS for that reason. There is no content management system to maintain and no build process standing between a finished draft and publication.

What belongs here

Expect practical essays, field notes, and carefully formed questions. Some pieces may begin with a computer problem. Others may examine a workplace process or a broader change in how we live with technology.

The common thread is a preference for signal over volume. New work will arrive when there is something worth saying.
