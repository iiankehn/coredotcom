Zen offers an alternative path for Arc-style browsing

Workspaces and an adaptable interface put browser organization back in focus.

By Iian KehnOctober 25, 20261 min read

Disclosure: CORE is actively developing Acute Web, its own browser for Android and iOS.

A new assessment at The Verge highlights Zen as an alternative for people drawn to Arc’s interface. It is a browser-design story rather than a new product launch.

Zen’s own website describes a free, open-source project with Workspaces for organizing tabs, a Compact Mode that hides the tab bar, and Split View for putting two pages beside each other.

The Verge’s Terrence O’Brien praises its organization but flags limitations, including DRM video playback in his testing. That qualification matters for anyone whose browser doubles as a streaming player.

The broader appeal is straightforward: a browser can compete through how it arranges everyday work. Zen’s pitch gives tab management and screen space a prominent role, with customization available through its Mods collection.
